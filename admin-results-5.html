Results & Trophy Tool

New to this tool? Here's the order to work in

  1. Add your entrants — upload the CSV or add people one at a time as late entries come in.
  2. Enter each class result as judging finishes, using the IDs from the entry cards. Joint placements just get more than one ID.
  3. Leave the trophies to work themselves out — every cup updates the moment you save a result. The Handicraft cups will ask you a quick question if they need to know someone's gender to decide a winner.
  4. At 4.30pm, hit "Print prize-giving sheet" for a single page listing all 41 trophies and their winners.

Everything is saved automatically in this browser as you go — see "Backup / sharing" below if you need to hand this over to someone else or use a different device.

5 Trophies

Computed automatically from the class results above, following Show Rule 8's points system (1st=5, 2nd=3, 3rd=2, HC=1) and tie-break (most 1st places, then most 2nd places). Trophies marked Judged aren't decided by points — they're the judge's choice of best exhibit, so just type the winner in in the box provided for your records and the prize-giving sheet. The two Handicraft cups are restricted by gender — if the top scorer's gender isn't known yet, you'll be asked right there, one person at a time, only when it actually affects the result.
